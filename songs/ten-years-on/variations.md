# ten-years-on — all variations


## acoustic — Ten Years On

Project: `work/ten-years-on-acoustic`  
Takes: `acoustic_take-1_Ten-Years-On.mp3`, `acoustic_take-2_Ten-Years-On.mp3`

**Style:** one repeating four-chord loop Bm - G - D - A, one chord per bar, the same four chords in the same order through the entire song and never anything else, bittersweet acoustic pop love song, 90 BPM, D major, fingerpicked nylon-string guitar up front with a slight Spanish feel, a second acoustic guitar strumming softly on the choruses, short Spanish-style guitar fills between vocal lines, cajon, soft handclaps, upright bass, warm emotional male tenor, close and breathy in the verses, aching and open in the chorus, gentle falsetto lifts, strong unforgettable vocal melody that lingers sweetly on the major third, big singable chorus hook, warm natural room sound, raw heartfelt performance, nostalgic and tender, quiet opening that builds to a full-hearted chorus


*Verse 1*  
Five a.m., the sea at Hua Hin like glass  
We'd swim out before the town woke up  
Fruit from the market in a plastic bag  
We'd eat it on the motorbike, and that was enough  

*Pre-Chorus*  
We were fire and fire, we could burn the whole night down  
Then water, the calmest sea around  

*Chorus*  
Ten years on, another life, another shore  
I don't think about the fights anymore  
I think about the roads, and the beaches we found  
Sitting side by side while the sun went down  
And every year they light the candles, and I think of you  
'Cause it's your birthday too  

*Verse 2*  
Forty kilometres on the motorbike  
Any makeshift room would do for the night  
We had next to nothing, and we had it all  
Another empty beach, watching the sun fall  

*Verse 3*  
We could do it cheap, we could do it grand  
You were just as happy with a street-food stand  
A few drinks deep, then home, and you'd put Faded on  
We'd be asleep before the end of the song  

*Pre-Chorus*  
We were fire and fire, I can still feel the heat  
Then water, that's the part I keep  

*Chorus*  
Ten years on, another life, another shore  
I don't think about the fights anymore  
I think about the roads, and the beaches we found  
Sitting side by side while the sun went down  
And every year they light the candles, and I think of you  
'Cause it's your birthday too  

*Verse 4*  
We built a house, it's yours now, and that's right  
I left, and it's the hardest thing I've done in my life  
I've been married since, and I'll tell you something true  
I don't think of her the way I think of you  

*Bridge*  
I'm in Australia now, I built a life, I'm fine  
I'm not asking for you back, I'm not rewriting time  
But if this ever finds you, wherever you are  
You'll know it's you, you were the best of it, by far  

*Final Chorus*  
Ten years on, another life, another shore  
I don't think about the fights anymore  
I think about the roads, and the beaches we found  
Sitting side by side while the sun went down  
And every year they light the candles, and I think of you  
'Cause it's your birthday too  
'Cause it's your birthday too  
It's your birthday too  

*Outro*  
Happy birthday to you  
And me
