# Ten Years On

A personal, true-story birthday song: memories of Thailand, told warm and plain, with nothing asking for anything back.
It shares a birthday with the person it's for — hence "it's your birthday too". Anonymous by design.

## How it was made

1. The sound and melody come from `ten-years-on-pluck` take 2, the take Sam loved. Every fresh re-performance
   changed the tune, so new words were put on it with a **Suno cover** of that take instead (12 credits a run).
2. The story verses about Bangkok and Australia went through many drafts and were dropped — each came out
   either crammed or corny. They were cut out of the recording itself (no regeneration): chorus 2 now jumps into
   the final chorus mid-line at a point where both are equally loud, and the closing "And me" was trimmed.
3. The chart was transcribed from that final edited recording, so it matches what is heard.
