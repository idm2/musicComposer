# Brief — "in the flow" love song

## What I want
An ORIGINAL love song in the same sound-world as the reference: the same warmth, tenderness and
"beautifulness" — intimate devotion that opens into a joyful, soaring chorus. It must sound like my own song,
not a copy: completely different words, a different melody, a related-but-different chord movement.

## What it is about
The focus is a partner — a girl, finding "the one" — because that is what connects with people. Around that:
- living life fully and then finding the one you love
- being "in the flow": when you are in the flow, you are in the flow, and everything just works
- spiritual energy, the universe, connection — touched on lightly, as imagery, never preachy or new-age jargon
- she is part of that flow: loving her like the loving energy of the universe, and never letting go

Keep it a love song first. The universe/energy/flow language should serve the love story (e.g. she is where the
current was carrying me all along), not replace it. Concrete, physical images (hands, light, water, streets,
morning) mixed with the bigger cosmic ones. Warm, sincere, a little awestruck. Second person ("you") to her.

## Measured facts about the reference (from DSP analysis, not guessed)
- Key F# major, about 91 BPM, 4/4.
- One four-chord loop, one chord per bar, the whole way through (vi – IV – I – V).
- Form heard: a sparse plucked intro, a hook sung over just the plucked part, then the beat and bass arrive for
  the verse, then a full joyful chorus with stacked harmonies.

## Direction for my song
- Tempo in the same relaxed mid-tempo pocket (84–92 BPM), 4/4.
- Centre it on a warm PLUCKED / FINGERPICKED GUITAR figure instead of a synth pluck, so I can play it on
  acoustic guitar — but keep the polished, modern, spacious production: deep soft bass, gentle electronic
  kick-and-snap beat that enters after the first hook, airy pads, clean wide reverb.
- Smooth male tenor lead, breathy falsetto lifts, thick stacked harmonies on the chorus.
- Structure: Intro (guitar alone) → soft Hook/Chorus over guitar only → Verse 1 (beat enters) → Pre-Chorus lift →
  full Chorus → Verse 2 → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge (strips back, most spiritual moment) → final big Chorus → Outro.
- The chorus hook should be short, singable and repeatable, built around the idea of being "in the flow" with her.
- Target length about 3 minutes (180 s).
