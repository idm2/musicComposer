# In the Flow

An original love song — finding "the one", and the feeling that when you are in the flow, everything just works.
Written to live in the same *sound-world* as the reference (warm, tender, polished, a plucked loop that opens into
a joyful stacked-harmony chorus) while being its own song: new words, new melody, a related-but-different chord movement,
a different key and tempo.

> **How to use this file.** Paste the **Style prompt** and **Lyrics (generator format)** blocks into Suno (Custom Mode)
> or another generator. Everything else — harmony, voicings, tab, arrangement — is the full musical brief for a human
> player, a producer, or a chord/tab tool.
>
> **Honest note on chords.** Song generators are prompt-driven, not score-driven: they will follow the key, tempo, mood
> and structure below quite well, but they will not reliably play *these exact chords*. The harmony here is the song as
> **written**. Once a take is generated and chosen, Song Composer transcribes that actual recording, so the final
> chords/tab match what you hear (that part of the tool is still being built — Tasks 15–27).

## 3. Tone, sound and production brief

- **Feel:** tender, warm, a little awestruck. Never heavy, never sad. Intimate in the verses, wide-open and joyful in the chorus.
- **Guitar:** fingerpicked steel-string acoustic, capo 2, played softly near the neck. Let the top two strings ring
  through every chord (see voicings) — that constant shimmer is the glue, like the reference's plucked loop.
  Clean, wide plate/hall reverb, a touch of stereo delay. No strumming until the first full chorus.
- **Bass:** deep, soft, rounded sub/synth bass, long notes on the chord roots, enters at Verse 1. No slap, no growl.
- **Beat:** gentle electronic kick + finger-snap (or soft rim) on 2 and 4, light closed hats in 8ths, enters at Verse 1,
  drops out for the bridge, returns for the final chorus. Nothing aggressive.
- **Pads:** airy, slow-attack pad that swells in each pre-chorus and sits under the choruses.
- **Lead vocal:** smooth male tenor, close-miked and breathy in the hook and verses, opening up in the chorus.
  Rhythmic, precise phrasing — mostly one lyric line per bar. Falsetto lifts on "flow" and "go".
- **Harmonies:** the chorus is made big by **vocal stacking**, not volume — doubles left and right, a third above,
  an octave-up falsetto on the last two lines. One answering harmony in Verse 2. Full stack plus ad-libs in the final chorus.
- **Mix:** lead centred, harmonies and guitar doubles panned wide, generous clean reverb, polished modern level.

## 4. Harmony

piano, bass or chord tool needs). One chord per bar unless shown otherwise.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Intro / Hook / Chorus / Outro | 4-bar loop | **Cadd9 – G – Em7 – Dsus4** | D(add9) – A – F♯m7 – Esus4 | IV – I – vi – V |
| Verse | 4-bar loop | **G – Bm7 – Em7 – Cadd9** | A – C♯m7 – F♯m7 – D(add9) | I – iii – vi – IV |
| Pre-Chorus | 4 bars | **Am7 – Cadd9 – Dsus4 – D** | Bm7 – D(add9) – Esus4 – E | ii – IV – Vsus – V |
| Bridge | 8 bars | **Em7 – Cadd9 – G – D/F♯ – Em7 – Cadd9 – Cm – Dsus4→D** | F♯m7 – D(add9) – A – E/G♯ – F♯m7 – D(add9) – **Dm** – Esus4→E | vi – IV – I – V/3 – vi – IV – **iv** – V |

Why it works: every loop uses the same four "home" chords as the reference's family (I, IV, V, vi), so it sits in the
same emotional world — but the **order** is different in every section, the verse adds the softer **iii** chord, and the
bridge's one bar of **minor iv** (Cm shape / Dm sounding) is the single dark colour in the song, resolving straight
back into the brightest chorus.
